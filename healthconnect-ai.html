THE CONCEPT / 01

Wellness with intention.

The proposal focuses on awareness and educational support, with appropriate permission-based clinic workflows.

01 / STUDENTS

Wellness tracking

Students could enter voluntary health and wellness information and review their own entry history.

02 / EDUCATION

General AI wellness tips

Planned non-diagnostic guidance about healthy habits and preventive self-care.

03 / HABITS

Reminders

Helpful prompts intended to encourage healthy everyday routines.

04 / ACCESS

Student records

Registration, access controls, and privacy-conscious management of health information.

05 / CLINIC

Authorized review

Clinic personnel could access permitted student records and coordinate appropriate follow-up.

06 / SAFETY

Rule-based alerts

Serious warning conditions are intended to be flagged using clinic-approved rules rather than AI guesses.

PLANNED TECHNOLOGY / 02

Designed as an app concept.

The proposal includes Flutter/Dart for mobile development, Django for backend services, PostgreSQL for data, and a planned OpenAI API integration for general, non-diagnostic wellness content.

Flutter / DartPython / DjangoPostgreSQLOpenAI API (planned)Rule-based alerts
IMPORTANT PROJECT NOTE

HealthConnect AI is a proposed capstone and illustrative interface concept, not a finished or clinically validated medical application. It is not intended to diagnose conditions, provide prescriptions, or replace emergency care and professional medical advice.
